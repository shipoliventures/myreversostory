The Archive

Every back
tells a story.

A prince in the Foreign Legion. A king who was never crowned. An aviator who vanished. A painting stolen and found in a stone wall. And, eventually, yours. This is the archive of what collectors and the manufacture itself have put on the second face of a Reverso.

Collector Stories

The reason this site exists. The pieces below were made for princes and museums; these were made for anniversaries, for fathers, for a village that isn't on the map anymore. Every one is submitted by its owner and read by a person before it appears.

II
1933 – 2009  ·  documented individual watches

Pièces Uniques

Watches made for one person, engraved or enamelled by the manufacture's own hands. Some of the owners are among the most famous people of the twentieth century. Some have never been identified at all — the engraving survived; the name did not.

III
1990s – 2026  ·  limited artistic editions

Artistic Editions

Where the case-back became a gallery. Grand feu enamel, miniature painting, guillochage, paillonnage and gem-setting from the Métiers Rares atelier — usually ten pieces, occasionally twenty, never many.

IV
1990s – today  ·  0 entries

Commemorative Editions

Watches whose case-backs were decorated to mark something — a club, a city, a boutique opening, an anniversary. Not simply watches made in small numbers: a limited complication belongs on the models page. What qualifies here is that the back was given a meaning.

The cards above are the ones whose case-backs were decorated. Many more Reversos were made in limited numbers with special dials, for particular markets and retailers, without a personalised back — those are catalogued separately.
