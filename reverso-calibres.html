The Calibres

The wrong shape
for everything.

Watchmaking is built around the circle. Gear trains are round, balance wheels are round, repeater gongs want to be coiled circles. The Reverso is a rectangle that turns over — and the fifty-odd movements built to live inside it are the record of what happens when a house refuses to accept that as a limitation.

Fifty Movements, One Problem

Since 1931, Jaeger-LeCoultre has created around fifty different calibres for the Reverso alone — from the smallest mechanisms it makes to some of the most complicated, each one moulded to the shape of a watch that has to work upside down and back to front. What follows is not a spec sheet. It is the order in which the problems were solved.

Still Being Written

Two new calibres in the last two years.

Cal. 953 in 2025 earned seven patents; cal. 834 finally put world time in a rectangle. The hardest problems in this collection are evidently still open.
