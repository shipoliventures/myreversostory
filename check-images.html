GitHub Pages treats filenames as case-sensitive; your Mac doesn't. A photo named A-Balbo-front.jpg sits happily in your folder, loads on your laptop, and returns nothing on the live site. This compares every filename the data files ask for against what's actually committed to the repository.

Fill in your username and repository, then run the check. Nothing is changed — this only reads.
